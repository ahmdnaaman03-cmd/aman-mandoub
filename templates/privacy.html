Privacy Policy for Aman El-Mandoob

Last Updated: July 2026

App Functionality & Purpose: Aman El-Mandoob is an automation and integration tool designed to streamline order confirmation, delivery coordination, and payment status verification between merchants and delivery couriers.

GPS Location Tracking Disclaimer

Important Notice: Aman El-Mandoob does NOT use, track, or collect real-time or background GPS location data from merchants, couriers, or end customers. All workflows operate strictly through automated data exchange, status updates, and secure reference tokens without requesting or accessing geographical location permissions.

Data Collection and Usage

We only collect and process minimal order-related data necessary to execute payment verification and delivery automation. This includes basic order identifiers (Order ID, amount, fulfillment status) used solely for generating secure verification links and QR codes.

Data Security & Compliance

We are committed to protecting store and customer data. We strictly comply with Shopify API terms and do not sell, share, or monetize any merchant or end-user data to third parties.
